Data Dictionary for Tab Database
								
SONG: title of the song
ARTIST: name of the artist or band
YEAR: year the song was released
TYPE: type of artist (Person/Band)
GENDER: gender of the lead vocalist
DURATION: duration of the song
LANGUAGE: language(s) of the song
TABBER: person who tabbed the song
SOURCE: source of the tab (old, off, new)
DATE: date the song was first played by Ukulele Tuesday
DIFFICULTY: difficulty level of the song (from 1 to 5)
SPECIAL BOOKS: books where the song is included
